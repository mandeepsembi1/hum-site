Hum Support

Hum is a small, considered app — and support is answered by the person who makes it.

Contact

Email mandeepsembi@gmail.com with anything: bugs, questions, feature ideas, refund help. Include your iOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened, and you'll get a faster fix.

Common questions

  • Where are my recordings? On your iPhone, in Hum's own storage — and in your encrypted iPhone backup. Hum has no servers.
  • Deleted something by accident? Library → Recently Deleted keeps recordings for 30 days.
  • Subscription help. Apple handles all billing: Settings → your name → Subscriptions to cancel or change plans. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
