Hum — Privacy Policy

**Effective:** 2026-05-04 **Version:** 1.0

This is the public privacy policy for Hum, the on-device voice memo app for iOS. The same text is available in Settings → Read the full privacy policy.

---

Hum never receives your voice.

Hum records audio, transcribes it entirely on your iPhone (using Apple's on-device speech engine or a locally-running Whisper model), and stores everything in this app's sandbox. Hum has no servers — audio is not uploaded to Hum, to OpenAI, or to anyone else.

Your recordings are included in your iPhone's own encrypted backup, so a lost or replaced phone doesn't lose them. That backup belongs to you and is managed by iOS — Hum never sees it.

On-device model downloads

Hum downloads its on-device model files from public CDNs on first use: the Whisper transcription model (and any model you switch to in Settings), the speaker-separation model used for multi-speaker recordings, and — if you enable "Advanced on-device Minutes" — a larger open-source language model (~2.5 GB, Wi-Fi only). These transfers contain only model weights — never your audio, transcripts, or identifiers. Once downloaded, everything runs locally on the device.

Optional: Live Researcher (off by default)

If you switch on the Live Researcher in Settings and add your own Anthropic API key, then while you record, short excerpts of the live transcript are sent to Anthropic (api.anthropic.com) using your key so it can look up facts in real time. That is the one and only feature that sends any of your content off the device, it never runs unless you turn it on, and your audio itself is never sent — only recent transcript text. Anthropic's own privacy terms apply to those requests. Turn the feature off and Hum goes back to fully on-device.

No analytics. No telemetry.

Hum collects no usage data, no crash reports, no event logs, no device identifiers. We do not know how often you record, when you record, or what you record. Hum's developer operates no servers that receive anything from you.

No account. No email.

Hum has no sign-in, no profile, no account system. You don't tell us your name. You don't tell us your email. You don't tell us anything.

AI features (Minutes & Chat)

Minutes (structured notes) and Chat use Apple Intelligence Foundation Models, which run on-device on iPhone 15 Pro and newer with Apple Intelligence enabled. The transcript text is processed locally by the on-device model and the result is returned to Hum. Nothing leaves the phone.

On older devices, these features surface an upgrade message — they are not silently routed to a cloud service.

Microphone permission

Hum asks for microphone access on first launch. iOS shows the standard permission prompt. If you decline, Hum cannot record — there is no fallback. Audio captured via the microphone is written to local storage only.

Subscription

Hum uses Apple's StoreKit for the subscription. Apple processes the payment and manages renewals — cancel anytime in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Hum never sees your card details, billing address, or Apple ID.

What we store, on this device only

Audio recordings (.caf and .m4a files), transcripts, summaries, folders, and embeddings used for search. Everything lives in the app's sandbox. Uninstalling Hum deletes all of it.

Sharing & exporting

When you tap Share or Export, you choose what leaves the device. Markdown files, plain text, Obsidian links, and Drafts links are sent to the destination you pick. We do not send copies anywhere.

Accuracy — please read

Hum transcribes and summarises automatically. It is not perfect. Accuracy depends entirely on the audio: distance from the microphone, background noise, music, accents, people talking over each other, and recording quality all affect the result. Transcripts, Minutes, and chat answers may contain errors, misheard words, or missing content.

Hum is provided "as is," with no warranty of accuracy or fitness for any purpose. Do not treat its output as a definitive, complete, or legal record. For anything that matters, check it against the original audio — your recording is always kept. To the fullest extent permitted by law, Hum and its developer accept no liability for any loss, damage, or decision arising from errors, inaccuracies, missing content, or reliance on anything Hum produces.

Recording is your responsibility

You are responsible for how you use Hum. Laws on recording conversations and meetings differ by country and region — some require the consent of everyone being recorded. It is your responsibility to know and follow the law where you are, and to obtain any consent required before you record. Hum and its developer are not responsible for the recordings you choose to make.

Children

Hum is suitable for any age. No data collection means no special children's policy is required.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app under Settings → Read the full privacy policy in the next app update, and the change will be summarised in the App Store "What's New" notes.
